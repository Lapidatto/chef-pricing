# User Flows

1. On the first launch, choose the workspace folder.
2. Register ingredients and their purchase prices.
3. Register in-house preparations and build their compositions.
4. Register dishes and build their recipe sheets.
5. Review cost, suggested price, and margin.
6. Use Validation after editing CSV files in Excel.
7. Export recipe sheets and reports from the Reports screen.

Ingredients and recipes are deactivated rather than deleted to preserve history
and existing references.
